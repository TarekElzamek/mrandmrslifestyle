About this exercise library

Every exercise here is demonstrated by Tarek or Chloe, the husband-and-wife coaching team behind Mr & Mrs Lifestyle. Tarek coaches 1-to-1 personal training at Nogs Gym in Al Quoz, Dubai, and both coach clients online around the world.

Each page explains how to do the exercise step by step, the muscles it works, the mistakes we see most often in the gym, and how many sets and reps to do for building muscle, getting stronger or losing fat.
