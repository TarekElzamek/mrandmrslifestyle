3. Subscription & Cancellation Policy

3.1 Monthly subscription

All Mr & Mrs Lifestyle online coaching packages are monthly subscriptions. You pay on the day you join. After that, your card is charged automatically on the same date each month (your “renewal date”) until you cancel.

3.2 No refunds

Payments are non-refundable. That includes part-used months, unused check-ins or sessions, and stopping the programme early. The only exceptions are where UAE law requires a refund, or where we made a billing mistake, such as charging you twice. Billing mistakes are always refunded in full.

3.3 Notice to cancel: 14 days

To cancel, tell us at least 14 days before your next renewal date.

  • If you give notice in time, you won’t be charged again. Your coaching continues until the end of the month you’ve already paid for.
  • If you give notice later than that, the next payment will still be taken, and that month is your final month.

Example: your renewal date is the 1st. Cancel by the 17th of the month before and you won’t be charged again. Cancel on the 20th and you’ll pay for one more month, then the subscription ends.

3.4 How to cancel

Email info@mrandmrslifestyle.com or message us on WhatsApp. We’ll confirm your cancellation in writing within 2 working days. The notice period counts from the day we receive your message.

3.5 Fixed-length programmes

The 12-week and 16-week programmes and the 8-week meal plan are billed monthly. They end automatically when the programme finishes, unless you choose to continue.

3.6 Price changes

If the price changes, we’ll give you at least 30 days’ notice before it applies to your subscription.

3.7 Failed payments

If a payment fails, coaching is paused until it’s paid.

3.8 If we end your subscription

If we have to stop coaching you for a reason that isn’t your fault, we’ll refund the unused part of that month.
