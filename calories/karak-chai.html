Questions we get asked

How many calories are in Karak Chai?

About 140 kcal for 1 cup (150 ml), with 4g protein, 20g carbs and 5g fat. Values vary between restaurants.

Can I eat Karak Chai and still lose weight?

Yes. Fat loss depends on your total calories across the day, not one food. Karak Chai is about 7% of a 2,000 kcal day. Ask for half sugar: about 90 kcal a cup.

How long does it take to burn off Karak Chai?

About 28 minutes of brisk walking for a 75 kg adult. It's easier to adjust the portion than to try to burn it off, which is why we build favourite foods into our clients' plans.
