How to cut calories at McDonald's

  • Skip the fries or share them: a small portion alone is 250 kcal.
  • Choose a zero-sugar drink: a regular soft drink can add 150 kcal or more.
  • Pick grilled-style or simpler burgers: the Beef Burger is 254 kcal, the Big Tasty is 793.
  • Ask for no mayo or sauce on chicken burgers to save around 100 kcal.

Questions we get asked

How many calories are in a Big Mac in the UAE?

A Big Mac at McDonald's UAE is 564 kcal, with 23 g protein, 46 g carbs and 30 g fat (224 g serving).

What is the lowest-calorie burger at McDonald's UAE?

The Beef Burger, at 254 kcal. The Cheeseburger is next at 305 kcal.

What is the highest-protein choice at McDonald's UAE?

The Big Tasty has the most protein (44 g) but also 793 kcal. For protein per calorie, Chicken Tenders (19 g protein for 245 kcal) and the Egg McMuffin (20 g for 315 kcal) are the best picks.

Can I eat McDonald's and still lose weight?

Yes, occasionally. Fat loss depends on your total calories across the day. A Big Mac with a zero-sugar drink and no fries is 564 kcal, which fits into most fat-loss days.
