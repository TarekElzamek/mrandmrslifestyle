How our clients get these results

Every photo on this page is a real Mr & Mrs Lifestyle client, coached either face-to-face in Dubai or through online coaching. No stock photos, no filters. Different goals, different starting points, but the same approach behind every result: a plan built for one person, followed consistently, with a coach checking in every week.

Fat loss

Most of our fat loss clients lose fat while still enjoying brunches, dinners and travel. They follow calorie and protein targets set for their body, strength train to keep their muscle, and increase their daily steps. Read our guide to losing fat in Dubai.

Muscle gain

Clients building muscle follow progressive strength programmes where every session is logged, eat enough protein every day and prioritise sleep. See why most men stop building muscle.

Body recomposition

Many clients lose fat and build muscle at the same time, so their shape changes even when the scale moves slowly. That's why we track photos, measurements and strength, not just weight.

Women's transformations

Chloe's clients build strength and shape through progressive, glute-focused training and nutrition that supports hormonal health, including brides preparing for their wedding and mums returning to training after birth. Read her guide to glute training for women.

Competition prep

Some clients take their training further and prepare for the stage, with carefully structured training, nutrition and peak-week planning.

How long does a transformation take?

Visible changes usually start within 8–12 weeks of consistent training and nutrition, and bigger transformations take longer. Your coach will give you an honest timeline on your free strategy call.
