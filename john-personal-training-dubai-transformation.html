John’s 12-Week Transformation: 18 kg of Body Fat Lost

Long working days. Demanding deadlines. A commitment that brought back his confidence.

John came to Mr & Mrs Lifestyle wanting to lose excess body fat, improve his physique and feel confident in himself again.

As an engineer, he had a demanding schedule. Long hours, project responsibilities and deadlines made it challenging to give his own fitness consistent attention. He needed a clear commitment to training that could fit alongside his career.

John chose our 20-session one-to-one personal training package at Nogs Gym in Dubai. Over 12 weeks, he committed to the process and achieved a remarkable result: 18 kg of body fat lost, tracked through InBody assessments alongside progress photographs.

The starting point: wanting to feel like himself again

At the beginning, John was unhappy with his physique and lacked confidence in how he looked. He wanted to feel leaner, see more muscular definition and feel proud of his body.

His job presented a practical challenge. Engineering work comes with responsibilities that do not disappear when it is time to train. After long days, finding the energy and time for the gym could be difficult.

For John, the turning point was making space for his own goals within that schedule. Booking personal training gave him dedicated time to focus on himself and a clear commitment to follow through on.

The process: showing up around a demanding career

Throughout the 12 weeks, John put consistent effort into his transformation. His 20 coached one-to-one sessions with Tarek at Nogs Gym, spread across the 12 weeks, provided focused time to train with Mr & Mrs Lifestyle and work towards his goal.

There were still long working days. There were still deadlines and responsibilities. John continued making time for training alongside them.

That commitment is an important part of his story. The photographs capture the physical result, but behind them were repeated decisions to turn up and put in the work.

How we tracked his progress

We monitored John’s transformation using InBody body-composition assessments and progress photographs.

The InBody assessments recorded an estimated 18 kg reduction in body fat over the 12-week period. Progress photographs provided a visual record of the changes in his physique, including a leaner waist and more visible muscular definition.

Using both methods helped document his progress beyond a single number on the scales.

InBody measurements are estimates and can vary with hydration and testing conditions.

The result: a transformed physique and renewed confidence

By the end of the 12 weeks, John had lost 18 kg of body fat and achieved a visibly leaner, more defined physique.

The personal change mattered just as much. As John described it to his coach, he could hardly believe the difference. He felt amazing, was happier with his body and had regained the confidence he had been missing.

Reaching the physique he had worked towards gave him a strong sense of achievement. He could see the result of the effort he had made alongside a demanding career.

A message from Tarek

“I’m proud of John for the effort behind this transformation. He had long working days, deadlines and responsibilities, and he still made time to train.

Seeing his physique change was rewarding. Seeing him happy with himself and confident again made it even more special.

Well done, brother. You worked hard for this.”

— Tarek, Mr & Mrs Lifestyle
