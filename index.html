Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does online coaching actually work?+
Once you purchase a package, you'll fill in our detailed onboarding questionnaire covering your goals, lifestyle, training history, dietary preferences and any injuries. Within 48 hours, you'll receive your plan: a fully custom training plan and nutrition guide on Elite, VIP and Couples, or the ready-made beginner programme with your calorie and macro targets on Economy. Depending on your package, you'll also get regular check-in calls and direct WhatsApp access to Tarek or Chloe.
Do I need a gym membership?+
No — we can build your plan around a gym, home equipment, or even no equipment at all. Just let us know your situation in the onboarding form and we'll design accordingly.
What results can I realistically expect?+
Results depend on consistency, starting point, and goal. Visible changes usually start within 8–12 weeks of consistent training and nutrition, and bigger transformations take longer. Our clients have lost between 5–20kg and gained between 3–10kg of muscle in 12-week cycles — with proper commitment to the plan.
I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?+
Absolutely. Many of our best transformations are from people who had never trained consistently before. We meet you where you are and build from there. Beginners often see the fastest results because every positive change is new for their body.
Can I train with you if I'm pregnant or postpartum?+
Yes — our team is certified in Pre and Postnatal Exercise. We offer safe, effective programming for every stage of pregnancy and postnatal recovery. Always consult your doctor before starting, and let us know your situation during onboarding. Chat with Chloe on WhatsApp →
How do I contact you between sessions?+
Elite and VIP clients have direct WhatsApp access to their coach with a guaranteed response within a few hours. Economy package clients can reach us via email. We're genuinely available — this is not a bot.
