How to Lose Fat in Dubai:
The Complete Guide for 2026

By Tarek Elzamek · Mr & Mrs Lifestyle · 8 min read · April 2026
← Back to Blog

Living in Dubai is incredible. The lifestyle, the energy, the food scene — there's nowhere quite like it. But if you're trying to lose fat, Dubai can feel like it's working against you at every turn. Endless brunches. Late-night shawarma runs. Business dinners you can't say no to. A gym culture that's inconsistent at best.

After coaching 500+ clients across Dubai and worldwide, Tarek and I have seen every obstacle there is. And we've helped people overcome all of them. This guide is everything we know about losing fat while actually living your life in Dubai — no crash diets, no skipping social events, no misery.

  • The Dubai restaurant strategy that lets you eat out and still lose fat
  • How to handle brunches, Ramadan and business dinners
  • The simple macro framework that drives results
  • The training approach that works around a Dubai lifestyle

Why Fat Loss Is Hard in Dubai (And Why It Doesn't Have to Be)

Dubai presents a unique set of challenges that most generic fitness advice completely ignores. Portion sizes at restaurants here are enormous. The social culture revolves around food and dining. The heat makes outdoor activity uncomfortable for half the year. And the fast-paced lifestyle means stress levels — and cortisol — are consistently high.

Add to that the fact that many residents are expats living away from family support systems, working long hours, and frequently travelling — and you have a recipe for inconsistency.

But here's what we've learned from coaching hundreds of Dubai residents: the environment isn't the problem. The strategy is. With the right approach, Dubai's lifestyle becomes manageable — and fat loss becomes sustainable.

Step 1: Understand Your Numbers

Before anything else, you need to understand your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) — the number of calories your body burns in a day. Fat loss happens when you eat slightly less than this number consistently over time.

A simple starting point: multiply your bodyweight in kg by 22 (for women) or 24 (for men) to get your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). Then multiply that by your activity level:

  • Sedentary (desk job, little movement): BMR × 1.2
  • Lightly active (1–3 workouts per week): BMR × 1.375
  • Moderately active (3–5 workouts per week): BMR × 1.55

That final number is roughly your TDEE. Eat 300–500 calories below it consistently, and you will lose fat. It really is that simple — the difficulty is execution in an environment like Dubai.

💡 Tarek's Rule

"Don't try to cut 1,000 calories a day. A 400-calorie daily deficit means 1kg of fat loss every 2.5 weeks. That's 5–6kg in 3 months — sustainable, real fat loss that doesn't destroy your muscle or your social life."

Step 2: Master Protein — The #1 Fat Loss Tool

Of everything we teach our clients, this single habit delivers more results than anything else: hit your protein target every single day.

Aim for 1.8–2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight. For a 75kg person, that's 135–165g of protein per day. Protein keeps you full, preserves your muscle as you lose fat, and requires more energy to digest than carbs or fat — meaning you burn more calories just processing it.

In Dubai, excellent protein sources are everywhere:

  • Grilled chicken, fish, and meat at virtually every restaurant
  • Eggs — available everywhere, prepared any way
  • Greek yoghurt and cottage cheese from any supermarket
  • Lean beef, turkey, and seafood from Carrefour, Spinneys, or Waitrose
  • Protein shakes as a convenient top-up

Make protein the anchor of every meal and everything else becomes easier.

Step 3: The Dubai Restaurant Strategy

This is where most people in Dubai struggle — and where our approach is completely different from generic diet advice. We don't tell our clients to avoid restaurants. We teach them to navigate them.

The Simple Rules for Eating Out in Dubai

  • Order protein first. Grilled chicken, fish, meat or eggs should be the anchor of your meal. Build everything else around it.
  • Ask for sauces on the side. Most of Dubai's restaurant calories are hiding in dressings and sauces. Get them on the side and use sparingly.
  • Eat to 80% full. It takes 20 minutes for your brain to register fullness. Stop before you feel stuffed.
  • Drink water before and during your meal. Thirst is frequently mistaken for hunger. Two glasses of water before eating will naturally reduce how much you eat.
  • At brunches — protein and salad first, treat dishes last. Fill up on the good stuff before you get to the indulgent section.
🍽️ Brunch Strategy

Dubai brunches are legendary — and they don't have to derail your progress. Eat a high-protein breakfast beforehand so you arrive not starving. At the brunch, fill your plate with protein and vegetables first. Enjoy the experience. You can absolutely have one indulgent meal per week and still lose fat — it's the other 20 meals that matter.

Step 4: Ramadan and Fat Loss

One of the most common questions we get is: "How do I maintain my progress during Ramadan?" The answer — with the right approach, Ramadan doesn't just preserve your results. Some of our most impressive client transformations happen during Ramadan.

  • Iftar: Break your fast with dates and water, then eat a protein-heavy meal. Grilled meat or chicken, vegetables, and a moderate amount of carbs. Avoid overdoing traditional fried foods immediately after breaking fast.
  • Suhoor: Focus on slow-digesting carbs (oats, whole grains), protein, and healthy fats. This combination gives sustained energy through the fast and prevents muscle breakdown.
  • Training: Move your workouts to the evening after Iftar when you have fuel in your system.

Step 5: Training That Fits a Dubai Lifestyle

The most effective training plan is the one you actually follow consistently. In Dubai, that means being realistic about your schedule, the heat, and your access to facilities.

3 sessions per week is enough to lose significant fat — especially when combined with proper nutrition. For fat loss, the most effective combination is:

  • Resistance training (weights): 2–3 sessions per week. Building and preserving muscle is the most powerful thing you can do for long-term fat loss.
  • Daily movement: 8,000–10,000 steps per day. In Dubai's malls and office buildings, this is very achievable.
  • One cardio session: 20–30 minutes of moderate intensity — indoor cycling, a treadmill walk, or swimming.

Step 6: The Mindset Shift That Changes Everything

The clients who achieve permanent fat loss don't think of it as a temporary diet. They make a fundamental identity shift: they decide they are a healthy, active person — before the results arrive.

Instead of "I'm trying to lose weight," they think "I'm someone who prioritises their health." Every healthy choice you make is a vote for your new identity. And votes compound over time.

The Bottom Line

Dubai's lifestyle doesn't have to be an obstacle to your fat loss. With the right strategy — understanding your numbers, hitting your protein, navigating restaurants smartly, and training consistently — you can lose fat while fully enjoying everything this city has to offer.

The difference between people who succeed and those who don't isn't genetics or schedule. It's having a clear plan and someone to hold you accountable to it. That's exactly what we do at Mr & Mrs Lifestyle.
