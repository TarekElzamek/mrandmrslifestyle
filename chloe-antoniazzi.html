Chloe Antoniazzi — Female Personal Trainer in Dubai

Certified personal trainer, glutes and pre/postnatal specialist, and qualified reformer Pilates instructor. Chloe helps women get stronger, shape their bodies and feel confident at every stage of life.

Message Chloe on WhatsApp Instagram @chloeanton3 →

In short: Chloe Antoniazzi is a female personal trainer and Pilates instructor in Dubai, co-founder of Mr & Mrs Lifestyle with her husband Tarek Elzamek. She coaches women privately at The Pilates Room by Chloe, her ladies-only studio in Meydan, and online worldwide.

Chloe's story

Chloe's passion for women's fitness comes from her own experience with body confidence, hormonal challenges and the confusion of conflicting advice.

Today she helps women train smarter, feel stronger and build a sustainable approach to their bodies at every stage of life. Her clients range from complete beginners who feel nervous in the gym, to brides with a deadline, to new mums rebuilding their strength after birth. What they have in common is a plan built around their body, their cycle and their life, with Chloe beside them every step.

She coaches at The Pilates Room by Chloe in Meydan: a private, ladies-only studio where women can train without feeling watched, and where mums can bring their baby along.

What Chloe specialises in

Glutes & lower body

Progressive, weight-based glute programming that actually changes shape. Read her guide to glute training for women.

Pre & postnatal training

Safe training through pregnancy and after birth, starting with breathing, core and pelvic floor-aware work before building load. Always with your doctor's clearance.

Reformer Pilates

Private reformer, mat and ball Pilates for core strength, posture and control, on its own or alongside strength training. Reformer Pilates in Meydan →

Women's nutrition

Eating to fuel training and support hormonal health, without extreme diets or cutting out the foods you love.

Questions about training with Chloe

Where does Chloe train clients in Dubai?

At The Pilates Room by Chloe, a private, ladies-only studio at Meydan Avenue, Block A, Shop 3, in Meydan (Nad Al Sheba 1), with free parking at the front and back.

What does Chloe specialise in?

Women's strength training and glute development, women's nutrition and hormonal health, pre and postnatal training, and mat, ball and reformer Pilates.

What qualifications does Chloe have?

Chloe is a certified personal trainer, glutes specialist, pre and postnatal specialist and nutrition specialist, and a qualified mat, ball and reformer Pilates instructor.

Can I bring my baby to my session?

Yes. Mums can bring their baby, and their nanny, to sessions at The Pilates Room.

How do I book a session with Chloe?

Book single sessions or 5, 10 and 20-session packages (1-to-1 or buddy) online through The Pilates Room by Chloe website, or message Chloe on WhatsApp.

Can I train with Chloe online?

Yes. Chloe coaches women online anywhere in the world, including her 12-week Booty Builder programme.
