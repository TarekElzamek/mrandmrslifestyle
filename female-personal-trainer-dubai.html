Why The Pilates Room feels different

It feels like home

Most studios feel like a studio. Ours feels like a living room: calm, warm and private from the moment you walk in. That's why we called it The Pilates Room.

Private only, never group classes

In group classes people of different levels train together and injuries happen. Here the full focus is on you, 1-to-1 or with one friend, with no rush.

Bring your baby, kids and nanny

Unlike most studios in Dubai, your little ones and your nanny are welcome to relax and wait while you train. And parking is free.

Pilates and strength in one room

Two reformer beds plus a smart strength machine with a touchscreen and up to 100 kg of resistance, so you can add weight training to your Pilates.

Coached by Chloe, who plans every session around your body, from glutes and strength to pregnancy, postnatal recovery and hormonal changes. Read her guide to glute training for women.

Ways to train with Chloe

Reformer Pilates with strength work

Every session is private and built around your goal, whether that's toning, getting stronger or feeling confident in your body again. Chloe blends reformer work with dumbbells and mat exercises, so you get the strength benefits without a gym floor.

Private Pilates in Dubai: your questions answered

Is there a private Pilates studio in Dubai?

Yes. The Pilates Room by Chloe is a private, ladies-only Pilates studio on Meydan Avenue (Block A, Shop 3) in Meydan, Dubai. Every session is private: just you and Chloe, or you and a friend, on the reformer, with no group classes.

Is this a group Pilates class?

No. We don't run group classes at all. Every session is private, either 1-to-1 or a buddy (2-to-1) session with a friend, sister or mum, so Chloe plans and corrects every exercise for you.

Do you have a female personal trainer in Dubai?

Yes, Chloe Antoniazzi is our female coach and reformer Pilates instructor. Rather than gym personal training, she offers private, ladies-only reformer Pilates at The Pilates Room by Chloe in Meydan, with dumbbell and mat strength work built into sessions, and she coaches women online worldwide.

Is the studio ladies only?

Yes. The Pilates Room by Chloe on Meydan Avenue is a ladies-only studio offering private sessions only, so there are no group classes and no one else training around you.

How much does private Pilates cost in Dubai?

At The Pilates Room by Chloe a single private session is AED 429. Packages bring the price down, starting from AED 365 per session with the 20-session package (AED 7,290). Buddy (2-to-1) sessions are also available if you'd like to share with a friend.

What packages are available?

You can book a single session or a 5, 10 or 20-session package, either 1-to-1 or as a buddy (2-to-1) session. Bigger packages bring the price per session down, from AED 429 for a single session to AED 365 per session on the 20-session package. See current prices on The Pilates Room by Chloe website or in the app.

How long is a private Pilates session?

Each private session lasts 50 minutes, so it fits easily into a busy day.

Can I bring my baby to my private Pilates session?

Yes. Babies are welcome at The Pilates Room by Chloe. Because every session is private and ladies only, you can train with your baby nearby, which makes it much easier to get back to exercise after giving birth.

Can my nanny come with me to Pilates?

Yes. Mums are welcome to bring their nanny along too, so your baby is looked after right next to you while you train.

When can I start training after having a baby?

Most women can start gentle, guided exercise once their doctor has cleared them, usually from around six weeks after birth, or later after a caesarean. Our team is certified in pre and postnatal exercise, and Chloe starts new mums with gentle, adapted reformer work focused on breathing, core and the pelvic floor before building up.

Is there free parking at the Pilates studio?

Yes. There is free parking at both the front and the back of the building on Meydan Avenue, so you never have to pay for parking or walk far, even with a pram.

Where is the Pilates studio?

The Pilates Room by Chloe is on Meydan Avenue, Block A, Shop 3, in Meydan (Nad Al Sheba 1), Dubai. Get directions on Google Maps.

Can I train with a friend?

Yes. As well as 1-to-1 sessions, Chloe offers buddy (2-to-1) private sessions, so you can train with a friend, sister or mum and share the cost.

I've never done Pilates before. Is private Pilates right for me?

Yes, private sessions are the easiest way to start. The reformer's springs can make every exercise lighter or harder, and Chloe teaches you the basics step by step, so complete beginners feel confident from the first session.

Is private Pilates worth it compared with group classes?

For most women, yes. In a private session the instructor watches every rep, corrects your form, adapts exercises for injuries, pregnancy or postnatal recovery, and works on your goals, which is hard to get in a busy group class. Many women find they see results faster.

What's the difference between reformer and mat Pilates?

Mat Pilates uses your bodyweight on the floor, while the reformer is a machine with a sliding carriage and springs that add support or resistance. Chloe teaches both, plus ball Pilates, and often blends them in one session.

Is reformer Pilates safe during pregnancy?

Pilates can be a good option during pregnancy and after birth when it's adapted properly. Our team is certified in pre and postnatal exercise, and you should always get clearance from your doctor first.

How often should I do reformer Pilates?

Two to three sessions a week is a great target if you want to see changes in strength, posture and tone within a few weeks. Even one private session a week, combined with walking or strength training, makes a real difference.

Can I combine Pilates with strength training?

Yes, and it works well. Sessions can include dumbbell and mat strength work, and the studio has a smart strength machine with up to 100 kg of resistance, so you can build strength and tone in the same session.
