Female trainer in Dubai: FAQ

Do you have a female personal trainer in Dubai?

Yes, Chloe Antoniazzi is our female coach and reformer Pilates instructor. Rather than gym personal training, she offers private, ladies-only reformer Pilates at The Pilates Room by Chloe in Meydan, with dumbbell and mat strength work built into sessions, and she coaches women online worldwide.

Is the studio ladies only?

Yes. The Pilates Room by Chloe on Meydan Avenue is a ladies-only studio offering private sessions only, so there are no group classes and no one else training around you.

Can I bring my baby to my session?

Yes. Mums are welcome to bring their baby, and even their nanny, to their session, which makes it much easier to get back to training after giving birth.

When can I start training after having a baby?

Most women can start gentle, guided exercise once their doctor has cleared them, usually from around six weeks after birth, or later after a caesarean. Our team is certified in pre and postnatal exercise, and Chloe starts new mums with gentle, adapted reformer work focused on breathing, core and the pelvic floor before building up.

Where is the studio and is there parking?

The Pilates Room by Chloe is on Meydan Avenue (Block A, Shop 3) in Meydan, Dubai, with free parking at the front and back of the building.

Can I train with a friend?

Yes. As well as 1-to-1 sessions, Chloe offers buddy (2-to-1) private sessions, so you can train with a friend, sister or mum and share the cost.

What packages are available?

You can book a single session or a 5, 10 or 20-session package, either 1-to-1 or as a buddy (2-to-1) session. Each session lasts 50 minutes. Bigger packages bring the price per session down. Packages are booked and paid online through The Pilates Room by Chloe website.

I don't live near Meydan. Can I still train with Chloe?

Yes. Chloe coaches women online wherever they live, with a custom training plan, nutrition guidance and check-ins. Her 12-week Booty Builder programme is AED 649, and she also coaches on the Elite and VIP online coaching packages.
