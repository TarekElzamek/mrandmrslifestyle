How to Do Bayesian Cable Curl

The Bayesian curl faces away from the cable so your arm starts behind your body, stretching the biceps fully. It's one of the best biceps builders around.

How to do Bayesian Cable Curl

  1. Set a cable low and hold a handle in one hand.
  2. Face away from the machine and step forward so your arm is pulled behind you.
  3. Keep your upper arm still behind your body.
  4. Curl the handle forward and up, squeezing the biceps.
  5. Lower slowly back into the stretch.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the elbow swing forward.
  • Too heavy, losing the stretch.
  • Leaning forward.

Coach's tips

  • Staggered stance helps you stay stable.
  • The stretch at the bottom is the key, so control it.

Sets and reps for your goal

Pick a weight where the last 1–2 reps of each set are hard but your form stays the same. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time.
