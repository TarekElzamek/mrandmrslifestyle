The One Identity Shift That Makes Transformations Permanent

✍ By Tarek & Chloe · 5 min read

In 10 years of coaching, we've seen hundreds of transformations. Some stick permanently. Others reverse within months. The difference has nothing to do with the quality of the plan, the amount of weight lost, or the intensity of the training. It has everything to do with one thing: identity.

← Back to Blog

Why Most Transformations Don't Last

Think about the people you know who have lost significant weight, only to regain it. What happened? The plan didn't fail. Their willpower didn't run out. What happened is that they never changed their identity. They saw themselves as someone who was 'trying to get fit' — not someone who was fit. And eventually, their behaviour caught up with their self-image.

James Clear describes this perfectly: 'Every action you take is a vote for the type of person you wish to become.' The most effective way to change your behaviour long-term is not to set a goal — it is to decide who you are.

The Identity Shift

The clients who transform permanently all make the same shift. They stop thinking 'I am trying to lose weight' and start thinking 'I am someone who takes care of their body.' They stop thinking 'I need to force myself to go to the gym' and start thinking 'I am the kind of person who trains.' They stop asking 'Can I eat this?' and start asking 'Is this what I eat?'

This sounds simple. It is not easy. It requires a conscious decision — and then thousands of small actions that reinforce that decision over time.

Step 1: Decide Who You Are Becoming

Write it down. Not a goal — an identity. Not 'I want to lose 10kg.' Write: 'I am someone who trains consistently and fuels my body well.' Read it every morning. It will feel uncomfortable at first. That discomfort is growth.

Step 2: Cast Votes for That Identity Every Day

Every time you train when you don't feel like it, you cast a vote for your new identity. Every time you choose protein over junk food, you cast a vote. Every time you get to bed on time, you cast a vote.

You don't need to be perfect. You need to win the majority of votes each day. Over time, the identity becomes undeniable — even to yourself.

Step 3: Protect Your Environment

Identity is fragile in unsupportive environments. Surround yourself with people who reinforce your new identity. In Dubai, this means choosing social settings that make healthy choices easier — having your gym bag packed the night before, keeping your kitchen stocked with food that aligns with your identity.

What We See in Our Coaching

The clients who transform permanently are the ones who — somewhere in weeks 4 to 8 — stop talking about what they're trying to do and start talking about who they are. They say 'I don't eat like that anymore' instead of 'I'm trying not to eat like that.' They say 'I train four times a week' as a statement of fact, not an aspiration.
