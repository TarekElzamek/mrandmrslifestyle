Questions

Is the workout tracker really free?

Yes. Anyone can use it for free: log workouts, track body weight and measurements, see progress charts and personal records.

Who can see my data?

Only you. If you train with Mr & Mrs Lifestyle, your coach can also see it so they can adjust your programme. Progress photos are stored privately and are never public.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It works in your phone's browser. For an app-like experience, add it to your home screen: on iPhone tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen"; on Android use the Chrome menu, then "Install app".
