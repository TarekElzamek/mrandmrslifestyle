Private Reformer Pilates in Dubai: Why 1-to-1 Beats Group Classes

By Chloe Antoniazzi  ·  5 min read  ·  October 2026
← Back to Blog

Walk into most Pilates studios in Dubai and you'll find a row of reformers, a full class, and one instructor trying to watch everyone at once. It works for some people. But if you've ever felt lost at the back of a class, unsure whether you were doing the movement right, there's a better way: private reformer Pilates.

At The Pilates Room by Chloe in Meydan, every session is private. No group classes, no mixed levels, no one watching. Just you, your instructor and a session built entirely around your body. Here's why we chose to do it this way, and what to expect.

What Is Private Reformer Pilates?

Reformer Pilates uses a machine with a sliding carriage, springs and straps. The springs can support you while you learn or add resistance as you get stronger, which is why the reformer suits complete beginners and experienced clients alike.

Private reformer Pilates simply means the session is yours. Instead of following a class plan written for a room full of people, your instructor chooses every exercise, adjusts every spring and corrects your form in real time, for you alone. At The Pilates Room you can book:

  • 1-to-1 sessions: just you and Chloe.
  • 2-to-1 (buddy) sessions: train with a friend, sister or mum and share the session.

Every session lasts 50 minutes.

Why We Don't Run Group Classes

This was a deliberate choice. In a typical group reformer class, beginners and advanced clients train side by side. The class has to move at one pace, so beginners are often pushed into movements they aren't ready for, while experienced clients don't get challenged enough. And with one instructor watching a whole room, small mistakes go unnoticed.

In a private session, everything changes:

  • The session is tailored to you. Your level, your goals, your body on the day.
  • All the attention is on you, or on you and one friend, never a full room.
  • You progress faster, because every minute is spent on what you need, and every rep is done properly.
  • It's safer. Your instructor sees every movement and adjusts it straight away, which lowers the risk of the strain and poor technique that can happen when a class moves too fast for you.
Chloe's Note

In a group, the class sets the pace. In a private session, you set the pace. That's the difference between going through the motions and actually getting stronger.

A Studio That Feels Like a Living Room

The Pilates Room doesn't feel like a typical gym or Pilates studio, and that's on purpose. It was designed to feel like a comfortable living room: a calm, private space where you can walk in, relax and feel completely at ease.

No mirrors full of strangers, no loud music, no rush between classes. Just a quiet, welcoming room where you can switch off from the day, focus on your body and feel safe doing it.

Ladies Only, Babies Welcome

The studio is ladies only, so you can train in complete privacy and comfort.

And because so many of our clients are mums, you're welcome to bring your baby, and your nanny too. No more skipping sessions because you couldn't arrange childcare, and no more putting your own health last.

Chloe is also a certified pre and postnatal specialist, so Pilates during pregnancy and after birth is adapted safely to each stage. Always check with your doctor before you start.

Benefits of Private Reformer Pilates

  • Core strength and stability that carries over into daily life and other training.
  • Better posture, especially if you spend long hours at a desk or carrying a little one.
  • Toning and control, particularly through the hips, glutes, thighs and core.
  • Flexibility and mobility through slow, controlled, full-range movement.
  • A calmer mind: focused breathing and a quiet space make it a real break from a busy Dubai day.
  • Better lifting: Pilates pairs perfectly with strength and glute training.

Who Is Private Pilates For?

  • Complete beginners who want to learn properly from the start, without feeling left behind in a class.
  • Women who feel uncomfortable in group settings or busy gyms.
  • Mums and mums-to-be who want safe, adapted sessions and the freedom to bring their baby.
  • Experienced clients who've plateaued in group classes and want to be properly challenged.
  • Friends, sisters and mothers and daughters who'd enjoy training together in a 2-to-1 session.

What to Expect at Your First Session

  1. A warm welcome. Make yourself comfortable. Bring your baby if you like.
  2. A quick chat about your goals, your experience and anything Chloe should know, such as pregnancy, recent birth or past injuries.
  3. An introduction to the reformer and how the springs and carriage work.
  4. Your 50-minute session, tailored to your level from the very first exercise.
  5. A plan for what's next, so every session builds on the last.
