How much does a personal trainer cost in Dubai?

Most 1-to-1 sessions in Dubai gyms cost between AED 200 and AED 600, depending on the trainer's experience, the gym and the size of the package. Home sessions usually cost AED 250 to 450. Two sessions a week for 12 weeks (24 sessions) therefore costs roughly AED 4,800 to AED 14,400 at a gym.

The price per session matters less than what you get for it. Before you pay, check that the trainer gives you a written plan, nutrition guidance and a way to track progress. Our full Dubai personal trainer cost guide explains what affects the price, and our checklist for choosing a trainer shows what to ask.

How many sessions do I need?

For most people, two to three sessions a week for at least 12 weeks is enough to see real change in body shape and strength. One session a week works if you also train on your own between sessions. Four or more is usually only needed for a fixed deadline such as a wedding or a competition.

Two to three sessions a week suits most people. It gives your body time to recover and is easier to keep up long term.
