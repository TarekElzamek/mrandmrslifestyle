How it works

  1. Free consultation. Message us on WhatsApp or apply online. We talk through your goals, schedule, training history and any injuries.
  2. Assessment & plan. Your coach assesses your movement and starting point, then builds your training plan and calorie and macro targets.
  3. 1-to-1 sessions. Train face-to-face with Tarek at Nogs Gym. Every session is logged so the weights, reps and difficulty keep progressing.
  4. Support between sessions. Nutrition guidance, check-ins and direct WhatsApp access keep you on track outside the gym.

Personal training vs online coaching

Not sure which is right for you? 1-to-1 training and personalised online coaching (Elite, VIP and Couples) both include a custom plan and direct coach access. The difference is whether your coach is next to you in the gym.

Personal training in Dubai: FAQ

How much does a personal trainer cost in Dubai?

Personal training prices in Dubai depend on the trainer's experience, the gym and how many sessions you book. Tarek offers 12-session and 20-session packages at Nogs Gym, starting from AED 3,800. Bigger packages bring the price per session down. If you want coaching at a lower cost, our online coaching starts from AED 299. Read our full guide to personal trainer prices in Dubai and our checklist for choosing a personal trainer in Dubai.

Where do your Dubai personal training sessions take place?

At Nogs Gym (Team Nogueira Dubai), Al Quoz, which is open to men and women. All of Tarek's 1-to-1 sessions take place at Nogs Gym. For ladies-only sessions, Chloe runs private reformer Pilates at The Pilates Room in Meydan.

Do you have a female personal trainer in Dubai?

Chloe Antoniazzi is our female coach and reformer Pilates instructor. She doesn't do gym personal training, but she offers private, ladies-only reformer Pilates at The Pilates Room in Meydan, with dumbbell and mat strength work built into sessions, and she coaches women online. Read her guide to glute training for women.

Is personal training suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Beginners often benefit most from 1-to-1 training because a coach teaches correct technique from day one and builds the programme around your current fitness, schedule and any injuries.

Can I train with a personal trainer during or after pregnancy?

Yes. Our team holds an Award in Pre & Postnatal Exercise Instructing, and Chloe offers adapted private reformer Pilates in Meydan and postnatal online coaching. Always get clearance from your doctor first, and tell us your situation at your consultation.

Is there a trial session?

Ask about a trial session when you book your free consultation. The consultation itself is free and there is no commitment.
