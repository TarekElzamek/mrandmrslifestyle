How it works

  1. Free consultation. Message us on WhatsApp or apply online. We talk through your goals, schedule, training history and any injuries.
  2. Assessment & plan. Your coach assesses your movement and starting point, then builds your training plan and calorie and macro targets.
  3. 1-to-1 sessions. Train face-to-face with Tarek or Chloe. Every session is logged so the weights, reps and difficulty keep progressing.
  4. Support between sessions. Nutrition guidance, check-ins and direct WhatsApp access keep you on track outside the gym.

Personal training in Dubai: FAQ

How much does a personal trainer cost in Dubai?

Personal training prices in Dubai depend on the trainer's experience, the gym and how many sessions you book. Tarek offers 12-session and 20-session packages at Nogs Gym, and Chloe offers single sessions plus 5, 10 and 20-session packages at The Pilates Room in Meydan. Bigger packages bring the price per session down. Message us for current package prices. If you want coaching at a lower cost, our online coaching starts from AED 299 per month.

Where do your Dubai personal training sessions take place?

At Nogs Gym in Dubai, which is open to all clients, and at The Pilates Room by Chloe in Meydan, a ladies-only studio where babies are welcome.

Do you have a female personal trainer in Dubai?

Yes. Coach Chloe Antoniazzi trains women 1-to-1 in Dubai (see ladies-only training). She specialises in glute development, women's nutrition and pre/postnatal training, and is a qualified mat, ball and reformer Pilates instructor. Read her guide to glute training for women.

Is personal training suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. Beginners often benefit most from 1-to-1 training because a coach teaches correct technique from day one and builds the programme around your current fitness, schedule and any injuries.

Can I train with a personal trainer during or after pregnancy?

Yes. Chloe is a certified pre and postnatal specialist and coaches women through pregnancy and postnatal recovery. Always get clearance from your doctor first, and tell us your situation at your consultation.

Is there a trial session?

Ask about a trial session when you book your free consultation. The consultation itself is free and there is no commitment.
