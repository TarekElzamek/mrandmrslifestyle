About Chloe, founder of The Pilates Room

“Pilates should feel personal.”

A private approach to movement, where every session is shaped around you, your body and your goals.

Chloe
Meet Chloe

Movement has always been part of my life.

Hi, I'm Chloe. Before moving into coaching, I trained professionally as a dancer and taught dance, which shaped the way I see movement today: not just how your body looks, but how it feels, how it moves and the confidence it gives you.

Over the years I've coached hundreds of women through different stages of life, from building strength and confidence to improving posture, pregnancy, postnatal recovery and simply finding time to focus on themselves.

The Pilates Room is the private space I always wished existed: calm, welcoming, completely personal and designed to make you feel at home.

My approach

Precision over repetition.

I believe the smallest details make the biggest difference. My approach is precise, personal and purposeful. I pay attention to how you move, not simply whether you can complete an exercise: your alignment, your technique and the way your body responds and progresses.

No two bodies move in exactly the same way, which is why every session is adapted around you.
