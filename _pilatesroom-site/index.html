Questions

Before your first session

Do I need previous Pilates experience?

No. A private session is the easiest place to begin. You will be shown the reformer, the springs and the straps at your own pace, with no group to keep up with.

How often should I do Reformer Pilates?

For the best results we generally recommend 2–3 sessions per week. The ideal frequency depends on your goals, experience and current training, and in your first session we'll help you decide what feels right.

What is a Buddy session?

The same personalised coaching, shared with one person you choose. Two reformers, one coach, with exercises and modifications tailored to each of you.

Can I do Reformer Pilates if I'm pregnant?

Reformer Pilates can be suitable during pregnancy with the right modifications. Chloe is a qualified Pre & Post Natal Exercise Specialist, so sessions are adapted to your stage of pregnancy and how you're feeling. We ask that you have medical clearance from your doctor before starting.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, fitted clothing you can move freely in. Grip socks are required; all other equipment is provided.

Do I need grip socks?

Yes, grip socks are required for all Reformer sessions. If you don't have a pair, The Pilates Room grip socks are available at the studio (AED 75).

How do I book?

Choose Book, pick your session or package and your preferred time, and send your request. Chloe confirms your time personally on WhatsApp.

What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?

You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your session. Inside the 24-hour window the session is charged, as the time is held exclusively for you.

Is The Pilates Room only for women?

Yes. The Pilates Room is exclusively for women: a ladies-only private space designed for comfort, privacy and personal attention.

Can I bring my baby?

Yes. Mums are welcome to bring their baby, and their nanny too. Just let Chloe know when you book.

When should I arrive?

Please arrive at your booked time and no earlier, as a private session may be taking place beforehand. There is no waiting area.

Where can I park?

Roadside parking is available directly outside The Pilates Room, as well as on the opposite side of the road.

What happens if my Buddy can't attend?

If one person can't attend but the other still comes, the booking remains a Buddy session and is charged as booked.
