Online coaching: FAQ

How do I get my workouts?

Through the My PT Hub app. After onboarding we connect you to My PT Hub, where you follow your assigned training programme, complete your check-ins and track your progress from your phone.

How does online coaching work?

After a free strategy call you complete a detailed onboarding questionnaire about your goals, lifestyle, training history, food preferences and injuries. Within 48 hours you are set up on the My PT Hub app with your training plan and nutrition guide. Depending on your package you also get weekly check-in calls, plan adjustments every two weeks and direct WhatsApp access to Tarek or Chloe.

How much does online coaching cost?

Prices start at AED 299 per month for the Economy Pack, a ready-made 4-week beginner programme. The Elite Coaching Experience is AED 899 per month and VIP Online Coaching is AED 1,999 per month. One-off programmes of 12–16 weeks start from AED 480, and the Couples Transformation is AED 1,499 per month.

Does online coaching work as well as an in-person personal trainer?

For most people, yes. Results come from a well-designed plan, nutrition and consistency, and online coaching provides all three with regular check-ins for accountability. In-person training helps most when you are new to lifting and need hands-on technique coaching.

Can I do online coaching if I don't live in Dubai?

Yes. We coach clients in the UAE, across the GCC, Europe and worldwide. Plans are built around your local gym, your home equipment or no equipment at all, and check-ins are arranged around your time zone.

Do I need a gym membership for online coaching?

No. We can build your plan around a gym, home equipment or bodyweight training. Tell us what you have access to during onboarding and the programme is designed around it.

Do you offer online coaching for women and new mums?

Yes. Coach Chloe Antoniazzi specialises in women's training, glute development, women's nutrition and pre/postnatal fitness, and coaches women online wherever they live. Chat with Chloe on WhatsApp →

Can my partner and I be coached together?

Yes. In the Couples Transformation programme Tarek coaches one partner and Chloe coaches the other, with individual plans and shared accountability check-ins.
