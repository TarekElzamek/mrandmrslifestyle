Online coaching suits you if you already have access to a gym or home equipment, want flexibility around work and travel, or live outside Dubai. Online coaching: FAQ

How does online coaching work?

After a free strategy call you complete a detailed onboarding questionnaire about your goals, lifestyle, training history, food preferences and injuries. Within 48 hours you receive a custom training plan and nutrition guide. Depending on your package you also get weekly check-in calls, plan adjustments every two weeks and direct WhatsApp access to Tarek or Chloe.

How much does online coaching cost?

Online coaching with Mr & Mrs Lifestyle starts at AED 299 per month for the Economy Pack. The Elite Coaching Experience is AED 899 per month and VIP Online Coaching is AED 1,999 per month. One-off 12-week programmes start from AED 480, and the Couples Transformation is AED 1,499 per month.

Does online coaching work as well as an in-person personal trainer?

For most people, yes. Results come from a well-designed plan, nutrition and consistency, and online coaching provides all three with regular check-ins for accountability. In-person training helps most when you are new to lifting and need hands-on technique coaching.

Can I do online coaching if I don't live in Dubai?

Yes. We coach clients in the UAE, across the GCC, Europe and worldwide. Plans are built around your local gym, your home equipment or no equipment at all, and check-ins are arranged around your time zone.

Do I need a gym membership for online coaching?

No. We can build your plan around a gym, home equipment or bodyweight training. Tell us what you have access to during onboarding and the programme is designed around it.

Do you offer online coaching for women and new mums?

Yes. Coach Chloe Antoniazzi specialises in women's training, glute development, women's nutrition and pre/postnatal fitness, and coaches women online wherever they live.

Can my partner and I be coached together?

Yes. In the Couples Transformation programme Tarek coaches one partner and Chloe coaches the other, with individual plans and shared accountability check-ins.
