Personal training in Meydan: FAQ

Where is the studio in Meydan?

The Pilates Room by Chloe is at Meydan Avenue, Block A, Shop 3, in Meydan (Nad Al Sheba 1), Dubai, with free parking at the front and back.

Is it really ladies only?

Yes. The studio is ladies only and every session is private, so there are no group classes and no one else training around you.

Can I bring my baby?

Yes. Mums can bring their baby, and their nanny, to their session.

How long are sessions and what packages are there?

Sessions are 50 minutes. You can book a single session or 5, 10 and 20-session packages, either 1-to-1 or buddy (2-to-1). Bigger packages lower the price per session.

How do I book?

Book and pay online through The Pilates Room by Chloe website, or message Chloe on WhatsApp if you'd like to ask questions first.

Do you train men in Meydan?

The Meydan studio is ladies only. Men can train 1-to-1 with Tarek at Nogs Gym in Al Quoz.
