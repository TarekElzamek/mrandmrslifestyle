Packages FAQ

Which package is right for me?+
The best way to find out is a free 20-minute strategy call — we'll ask about your goal, lifestyle, and budget and give you an honest recommendation. Most people who want real, fast results choose Elite. If you're on a tight budget, Economy is a great start. If you want the absolute best, VIP.
How much does a personal trainer cost in Dubai with you?+
1-to-1 personal training with Tarek at Nogs Gym, Al Quoz, is AED 3,800 for 12 sessions (about AED 317 per session) or AED 5,500 for 20 sessions (AED 275 per session). A single session is AED 400. If you'd rather train on your own with a coach guiding you, online coaching starts from AED 299 per month.
What's included in 1-to-1 personal training?+
A free consultation and movement assessment, a personal training plan with calorie and macro targets, hands-on coaching every session, a progress review and plan update every 4 weeks, and WhatsApp support between sessions.
Do you offer personal training for women?+
Yes. Tarek trains men and women at Nogs Gym. If you prefer a female coach and a private, ladies-only space, Chloe offers 1-to-1 reformer Pilates with strength work at The Pilates Room in Meydan.
Can I upgrade my package later?+
Yes, absolutely. Many clients start on Economy or Elite and upgrade as they see results and want more support. Just message us and we'll sort it out — you only pay the difference.
Is there a minimum commitment period?+
Every online package except the Economy Pack is a 12-week programme, billed monthly for 3 months and ending automatically when it finishes. The Economy Pack is a monthly subscription that renews until you cancel with 14 days’ notice before your next renewal date. Payments are non-refundable; see our Subscription & Cancellation Policy.
Do I need a gym membership?+
No — we build your plan around what you have. Gym, home equipment, or no equipment. Just let us know during onboarding and we'll design accordingly.
How quickly will I receive my plan?+
Within 48 hours of signing up and completing your onboarding questionnaire, you will be set up on the My PT Hub app, where your training programme, check-ins and progress tracking all live.
What's the difference between the specialist programmes and personalised coaching?+
The specialist programmes (Booty Builder, Muscle Gain, Fat Burn and the Tailor-Made Nutrition Plan) give you a focused programme to follow on your own, with email support and no coach check-ins. The Economy Pack gives you a ready-made 4-week beginner programme with email support. Elite, VIP and Couples give you 12 weeks of personalised coaching, regular check-ins and plan adjustments as you progress. All of these are 12-week programmes billed monthly for 3 months that end automatically. Only the Economy Pack is a monthly subscription that renews until you cancel with 14 days’ notice.
