Packages FAQ

Which package is right for me?+
The best way to find out is a free 20-minute strategy call — we'll ask about your goal, lifestyle, and budget and give you an honest recommendation. Most people who want real, fast results choose Elite. If you're on a tight budget, Economy is a great start. If you want the absolute best, VIP.
Can I upgrade my package later?+
Yes, absolutely. Many clients start on Economy or Elite and upgrade as they see results and want more support. Just message us and we'll sort it out — you only pay the difference.
Is there a minimum commitment period?+
No long-term contracts. Our packages are monthly and you can cancel anytime. We recommend at least 12 weeks to see significant results, but there's no obligation beyond the current month.
Do I need a gym membership?+
No — we build your plan around what you have. Gym, home equipment, or no equipment. Just let us know during onboarding and we'll design accordingly.
How quickly will I receive my plan?+
Within 48 hours of signing up and completing your onboarding questionnaire, your full custom training and nutrition plan will be delivered to your inbox.
What's the difference between a one-time programme and monthly coaching?+
One-time programmes (Booty Builder, Muscle Gain, Fat Burn) give you a fixed programme to follow on your own. Monthly coaching (Economy, Elite, VIP) gives you ongoing personalised support, regular check-ins, and plan adjustments as you progress.
