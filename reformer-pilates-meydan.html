Reformer, mat & ball Pilates

Reformer Pilates

A machine with a sliding carriage and adjustable springs. The springs can support you while you learn or add resistance as you get stronger, which makes it suitable for complete beginners and experienced clients alike.

Mat Pilates

Bodyweight Pilates on the mat, the foundation of core control and great to keep up at home between sessions.

Ball Pilates

Using a stability ball to challenge balance and core strength in new ways.

Reformer Pilates in Meydan: FAQ

Do you offer group reformer classes?

No. Every session at The Pilates Room by Chloe is private, 1-to-1 or buddy (2-to-1), so the whole session is tailored to you.

Is reformer Pilates good for beginners?

Yes. The reformer's springs can make movements easier or harder, so complete beginners can start safely while experienced clients are still challenged. In a private session Chloe adjusts every exercise to your level.

Is reformer Pilates safe during pregnancy or after birth?

Pilates can be a good option before and after birth when it's adapted properly. Our team is certified in pre and postnatal exercise, and you should always get clearance from your doctor first.

What's the difference between reformer and mat Pilates?

Mat Pilates uses your bodyweight on the floor, while the reformer is a machine with a sliding carriage and springs that add support or resistance. Chloe teaches both, plus ball Pilates.

Can I combine Pilates with strength training?

Yes, and it works well. Many clients mix reformer sessions with strength and glute training for core control, posture and better lifting technique.

Who can book?

The studio is ladies only. Sessions are 50 minutes, with single sessions and 5, 10 and 20-session packages, booked in The Pilates Room by Chloe app, online or via WhatsApp.

Where can I do private reformer Pilates in Dubai?

At The Pilates Room by Chloe on Meydan Avenue in Meydan, Dubai. It's a ladies-only studio where every reformer session is private, 1-to-1 or with one friend, taught by Chloe Antoniazzi.

How much is a private reformer Pilates session in Dubai?

A single private reformer session is AED 429, and packages start from AED 365 per session with the 20-session package (AED 7,290). 5 and 10-session packages are also available.

How long is a reformer Pilates session?

50 minutes, fully private, so every minute is spent on your own programme.

How often should I do reformer Pilates?

Two to three sessions a week is a great target if you want to see changes in strength, posture and tone within a few weeks. Even one private session a week, combined with walking or strength training, makes a real difference.

Can I bring my baby or nanny to reformer Pilates?

Yes. Babies and nannies are welcome at the studio, which is why so many new mums train with Chloe.

Is there parking at The Pilates Room?

Yes, free parking at the front and back of the building on Meydan Avenue.

Can I do reformer Pilates with a friend?

Yes. Buddy (2-to-1) sessions let you train with a friend, sister or mum in a private session and share the cost.
